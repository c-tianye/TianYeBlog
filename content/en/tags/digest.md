The "Digest" series is produced by [`worker/digest`](https://github.com/c-tianye/TianYeBlog/tree/main/worker/digest): a scheduled Cloudflare Worker crawls public sources, Gemini writes a bilingual summary, and the result is committed back to this repository.

Summaries can lag behind or misread the originals — always trust the linked source.
