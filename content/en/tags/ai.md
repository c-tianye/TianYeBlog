Every 2 hours: official updates from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI, Google DeepMind and Hugging Face, plus new AI papers on arXiv (cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL).

Lab posts are reported as the labs state them; **arXiv entries are preprints that have not been peer reviewed** and are labelled as such — a paper proposing something is not the same as a shipped product.
