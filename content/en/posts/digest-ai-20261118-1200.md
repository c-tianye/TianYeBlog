## Papers

### [Know When to Zoom: How Small Is Too Small for Vision-Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22709v1)

A new arXiv preprint investigates why vision-language models frequently miss fine visual details in large images. The study benchmarks multiple commercial models to measure detail loss caused by automatic image downscaling and rejection.

### [Making the World React Realistically: A Closed-Loop Interactive World Model for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22691v1)

A new preprint introduces a closed-loop interactive world model for autonomous driving. By coupling agent behavior modeling with visual generation, it balances behavioral realism and visual fidelity in simulated driving scenarios.

### [Remembered as Brief: Stored Preferences SilentlyRemove Decision-Critical Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22697v1)

A new preprint highlights safety risks in long-term AI memory, demonstrating how stored user preferences can silently strip decision-critical warnings in sensitive contexts like health and finance. The authors propose a dedicated benchmark to evaluate these omissions.

### [Worldviews in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22670v1)

A new preprint examines the underlying worldviews of large language models using established psychological surveys. The authors administer human psychology questionnaires to multiple models to map their implicit beliefs about reality.
