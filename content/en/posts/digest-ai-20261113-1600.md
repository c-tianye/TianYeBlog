### [Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334v1)

A new paper dissects the design choices of vision encoders in multimodal language models, examining the roles of visual priors, dedicated capacity, and fusion placement.

### [Classical Energies Learn to Reason 1,000x Faster than Looped Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17309v1)

A new paper proposes an energy-descent reasoning paradigm to overcome the instability and heavy compute costs of looped reasoning models.

### [Learning When Diffusion Language Models Should Regenerate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17320v1)

A new paper introduces DiVR, a verifier that helps diffusion language models predict generation errors and determine optimal token remasking strategies.

### [AgentSSL: Can MLE Agents Leverage Unlabeled Data?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323v1)

A new paper investigates whether machine learning engineering agents can effectively utilize unlabeled data under semi-supervised learning settings.

### [Metal Sandbox: Isolated Execution and Command Scheduling for GPU Coding Agents on Apple Silicon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17294v1)

A new paper presents Metal Sandbox, an execution and scheduling framework designed to enable concurrent GPU coding agent sessions on Apple Silicon.
