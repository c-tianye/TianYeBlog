## Papers

### [Direct Feedback Policy Optimization for AI Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29193v1)

A new preprint introduces DFPO, a single-trajectory reinforcement learning algorithm designed for AI coding agents to overcome the limitations of sparse terminal rewards in multi-turn interactions.

### [GameAnything: From Any Inspirations to Playable Game Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29238v1)

Researchers present an open-source agentic platform in a recent paper that converts arbitrary multimodal inspirations into interactive, playable game worlds.

### [Seeing Is Not Acting: WARD Diagnoses the Evidence-to-State-to-Action Gap in Longitudinal Clinical Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29243v1)

Addressing evaluation gaps in AI clinical care, a new paper proposes WARD, a benchmark diagnosing how medical agents update internal states and make decisions as patient evidence accumulates over time.

### [Tenlai-TTS 2.0: Continuous-Representation LLM-Based TTS System with End-to-End Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29226v1)

A preprint proposes Tenlai-TTS 2.0, an autoregressive LLM-based TTS system trained end-to-end with continuous speech latents to eliminate quantization information loss.

### [loopxDINO: Dense Visual Prediction via Iterative Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29180v1)

A new paper presents loopxDINO, replacing deep feedforward decoders with iterative latent reasoning to reduce activation memory overhead in dense visual prediction tasks.
