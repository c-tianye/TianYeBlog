A new paper introduces RobustRLlib, a unified library and benchmark designed to address limited algorithm reusability and unclear empirical capacity boundaries in robust reinforcement learning.

### [DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32969v1)

A new paper proposes DexPort, a hand-held skill interface aimed at resolving the central bottleneck of collecting high-quality demonstrations for dexterous manipulation.

### [Flownomaly: Single-Step Conditional-Mean Recovery for Few-Shot Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32964v1)

A new paper presents Flownomaly, which casts few-shot industrial anomaly detection as normal-counterpart recovery within a frozen feature space via conditional-mean recovery.

### [Measure Before You Train: Typed Decision Models vs. a Classical Baseline vs. a Generative LLM for Routing a Personal Multilingual Inbox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32955v1)

A new paper examines personal multilingual inbox routing and establishes a measurement protocol to evaluate different decision models under practical conditions.

### [Where Rounding Happens Matters: Rounding-Location Alignment Stabilizes LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32951v1)

A new paper investigates rounding-location alignment in large language model reinforcement learning to stabilize training against implementation-induced distribution shifts.

### [Knowledge Distillation across Item and Code Spaces for Parallel Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32943v1)

A new paper introduces a knowledge distillation approach across item and code spaces to improve parallel generative recommendation systems.
