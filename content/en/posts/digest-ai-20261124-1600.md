## Overview

This edition features the latest preprint research from arXiv. Highlights include structured pruning for autonomous driving VLAs, image generation via VLM representations, in-situ uncertainty monitoring in LLMs, and automated tool creation for multimodal search agents.

## Papers

### [Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31240v1)

A new paper explores structured pruning for vision-language-action (VLA) autonomous driving models. The authors analyze how to preserve both scene understanding and trajectory prediction while reducing computational and memory costs.

### [Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31237v1)

A new paper proposes leveraging representations from pretrained vision-language models to guide visual generation. The study addresses the challenge of making learned multimodal knowledge accessible to image generation modules within unified architectures.

### [Language Models Steer on Injected Uncertainty Tokens, but Fail to Self-Monitor In Situ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31228v1)

A new paper investigates LLM self-monitoring and confidence control during agentic reasoning. It finds that while injecting uncertainty tokens successfully steers models to repair errors, models struggle to accurately evaluate their own confidence in real time.

### [ToolSmith: An Automatic Tool-Generation Framework for Multimodal Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31198v1)

A new paper introduces ToolSmith, a framework for automatically generating search and visual tools for multimodal agents. The approach enables agents to dynamically adapt to unseen query types and failure modes beyond rigid hand-crafted tool libraries.
