## Overview

This edition highlights recent arXiv preprints focusing on deep mechanistic insights and practical agent deployments. Key topics include action-level evaluation for direct robot control, iterative game tuning driven by agent play experience, and internal concept selection in vision-language models.

## Papers

### [PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26624v1)

A new preprint introduces PlayCraft, an automated framework that uses vision-based agents to play games, gather gameplay insights, and iteratively revise game mechanics to enhance player mastery and engagement.

### [AgentRoboCritic: Understanding the Capability of Frontier Agents on Direct Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613v1)

This paper conducts a fine-grained, action-level evaluation of frontier multimodal language agents directly executing low-level robot control, bridging the gap left by standard episode-level metrics.

### [SoK: Assessing the Evolution of LLM Capability in Software Vulnerability Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26601v1)

A new research paper proposes a unified autonomy rating framework to benchmark the evolving capabilities of LLM agents in discovering, validating, and patching software vulnerabilities.

### [PGX: Physically Grounded Execution for Long-Horizon Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26596v1)

This preprint presents PGX, a framework that offloads temporal and precision control to classical training-free components to simplify long-horizon humanoid loco-manipulation.

### [Realized versus Population Reliability: Anytime-Valid Self-Consistency Certification under Correlated Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26592v1)

Addressing the fact that reasoning paths in language models are positively correlated, this paper introduces an anytime-valid self-consistency certification framework with guaranteed error control.

### [Probing the "Conscious" Mind: Selective Access and Global Workspace in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26590v1)

Based on Global Workspace Theory, a new preprint introduces the Multimodal Concept Lens (MC-Lens) to inspect internal visual and textual concept integration inside vision-language models.

## Takeaway

Frontier AI research is increasingly shifting toward fine-grained action analysis, closed-loop agent design, and interpretable internal model representations.
