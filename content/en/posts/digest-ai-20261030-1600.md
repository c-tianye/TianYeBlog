## Overview

This update highlights recent research preprints from arXiv. Key areas of exploration include the mechanics behind shallow safety alignment in multi-turn settings, efficient token routing for pixel-space generative Transformers, and deterministic ODE formulations for discrete diffusion.

## Papers

### [ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40627v1)

This preprint identifies that safety alignment in large language models is often concentrated in the initial output tokens, explaining multi-turn jailbreak vulnerabilities and introducing a targeted defense strategy based on this observation.

### [world2vec: Mechanistic world models through latent dynamics learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40637v1)

Addressing the fact that world models often solve control tasks without learning true physics, this paper proposes world2vec to extract mechanistic latent dynamics directly from observations and actions without reward signals.

### [Read, Allocate, Route: Role-Coupled Tokenization for Pixel-Space Generative Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40647v1)

To curb the quadratic cost of global attention in pixel-space generative Transformers, this study presents SpecSlot, decoupling global low-frequency structure from localized high-frequency detail for more efficient image generation.

### [An ODE-based Sampler for Discrete Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40618v1)

This paper introduces exponential residual flows to establish a deterministic ordinary differential equation framework for discrete diffusion models, paving the way for accelerated sampling and consistency distillation in discrete spaces.

### [Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649v1)

Revisiting the Neural Thickets hypothesis, this preprint demonstrates that reported gains from Gaussian weight perturbations do not require intricate geometric explanations and can be largely matched through simpler perturbations.
