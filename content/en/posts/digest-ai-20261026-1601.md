## Papers

### [SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32978v1)

A new paper introduces SceneBloom, an agentic framework that combines semantic planning with visual generative priors to generate realistic, physically valid 3D indoor environments for embodied AI training.

### [Beyond Discrete Tokens: Preserving Numerical Continuity in Medical Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32948v1)

A new paper addresses the mismatch between discrete language tokens and continuous measurement values in medical VLMs by proposing HAD-VLM, a hybrid autoregressive-diffusion architecture.

### [Monetizing Intelligence: Advertising and Subscription Design on LLM Platforms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32945v1)

A new paper models the economic choices of LLM platforms, analyzing how operators should balance model response quality, advertising intensity, and subscription pricing.
