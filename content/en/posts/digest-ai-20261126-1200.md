This update highlights recent preprint research focusing on representation learning, multi-agent systems, video generation, and domain-specific agent applications.

## Papers

### [RAP: Radial-Angular Random Projection for Hyperbolic Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34298v1)

— A new paper proposes random projection techniques tailored for hyperbolic spaces to address dimensionality reduction needs in modern Transformers and language models.

### [LatENSO v1.0: a learned latent state of the tropical Pacific for seasonal ENSO operators and forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34296v1)

— A new paper introduces a machine learning forecast system for the tropical Pacific using a Swin-transformer autoencoder to compress ocean and atmosphere fields into latent states.

### [WebVibe: Multimodal Interactive Website Generation via Reverse Synthesis and Visual Self-Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34292v1)

— A new paper presents an approach for generating interactive websites through reverse synthesis and visual self-distillation to handle multi-page routing and state transitions.

### [StateWeaver: Multi-Agent Coordination of Story-State Transitions for Long-Horizon Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34290v1)

— A new paper introduces a multi-agent framework combining active states and hierarchical collaboration to preserve consistency and event state changes in long video generation.

### [Latent-in-the-Middle: On the Security of Cross-Model Latent Communication in Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34278v1)

— A new paper analyzes the security risks of cross-model latent communication in heterogeneous multi-agent systems, particularly regarding KV cache transfers.

### [Beat This! Live - Online Beat and Downbeat Tracking by Predicting the Future](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34277v1)

— A new paper adapts an offline beat tracking model into a causal variant for real-time, low-latency online music beat and downbeat tracking.

### [Scaling Looped MoE Language Models under Matched Budgets: Information Flow and Parameter Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34276v1)

— A new paper investigates information flow and parameter configuration strategies for looped mixture-of-experts language models under strict resource constraints.

### [LatentCP-DPO: Conformal Pessimism for DPO with Finite-Sample Guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34275v1)

— A new paper combines conformal prediction with pessimistic preference optimization to provide finite-sample guarantees and prevent overfitting in direct preference optimization.

### [Libtt: A Compiler and Runtime for Tenstorrent Processors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34273v1)

— A new paper details a compiler and runtime stack that brings the PyTorch and JAX ecosystem to Tenstorrent hardware accelerators.

### [LLM-coupled interpretable tool wear monitoring with multi-evidence reasoning in CNC machining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34270v1)

— A new paper proposes a two-layer framework coupling deep learning and large language models to enhance interpretable tool wear monitoring in CNC machining.

### [Crucible: Evaluating LLM Agents in Executable Chemistry Research Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34254v1)

— A new paper introduces a comprehensive benchmark to evaluate large language model agents across all stages of the chemistry research design-make-test-analyze cycle.

### [MedSkill-E$^{2}$: Evaluating and Evolving Skills for Medical Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34250v1)

— A new paper investigates the evaluation and evolution of skills for medical agents to overcome underuse phenomena during autonomous task execution.
