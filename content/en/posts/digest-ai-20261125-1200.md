### [CPU-Eval: Evaluating Large Language Models for CPU Hardware Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32405v1)

Addressing headroom saturation in benchmarks like VerilogEval, a new paper introduces CPU-Eval to rigorously benchmark LLMs on complex CPU hardware code generation.

### [Fractal Neural Synthesis: Constant-Storage Procedural Weight Derivation via Complex Quadratic Boundary Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32393v1)

A new paper proposes Fractal Neural Synthesis, deriving network weights procedurally from a constant seed using complex dynamics to drastically cut storage on edge microcontrollers.

### [Verify then Steer: Progress Hallucination-Aware Recovery for Continuous Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32400v1)

To help navigation agents recover from route deviations, a new paper identifies the phenomenon of "progress hallucination" and proposes a verify-then-steer recovery framework.

### [Evolve to Learn: Gradient-aligned experience evolution for model self-improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32390v1)

A new paper introduces a gradient-aligned experience evolution framework to effectively internalize test-time interaction and reflection into model weights for self-improvement.
