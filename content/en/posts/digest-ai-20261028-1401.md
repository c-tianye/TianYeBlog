## Papers

### [Honeycomb-Surfels: Compact Hexagonal Primitives for Efficient Scene Rendering and Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37668v1)

A new paper introduces planar hexagonal surface elements (Hexels) for efficient 3D scene and 2D image representation, addressing memory and computational limits in resource-constrained settings.

### [ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666v1)

A new paper presents ThinkWorld to tackle the generalization bottleneck in Vision-Language-Action models by leveraging broad human video experiences instead of relying solely on action-labeled robot demonstrations.

### [When Should We Intervene at Test Time?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37658v1)

A new paper investigates test-time adaptation and addresses the challenge of deciding when to intervene to prevent performance degradation caused by shifting inputs.

### [Attention as Lattice Gauge Theory: Relations Interact Only Through Wilson Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37656v1)

A new paper connects attention mechanisms to lattice gauge theory, demonstrating that relations interact exclusively through Wilson loops under per-entity frame freedom.

### [KPPax: preprocess differentiable kinetic ODE for machine learning in atmospheric chemistry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37655v1)

A new paper introduces KPPax, a kinetic preprocessor that translates atmospheric chemical mechanisms into differentiable, JIT-compiled, and vectorizable code in JAX.

### [Pushing Dexterity and Safety for In-Hand Object Pose Reaching That Works Remarkably Well](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37652v1)

A new paper explores in-hand object pose reaching with multi-fingered hands, emphasizing the critical balance between diverse manipulation dexterity and safety.

### [Scaling Laws for Tool-Use Agents via Controllable Environment Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37648v1)

A new paper examines how data composition in synthetically generated tool-use environments impacts the training and scaling performance of large language model agents.

### [MuonGS: Pretraining LLMs with Relaxed Weight-Norm Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37647v1)

A new paper analyzes weight-norm control across language model pretraining trajectories using the accumulated effective learning rate.
