## Papers

### [LGMT: A Tool for Testing the Logical Reasoning Reliability of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32627v1)

This new preprint introduces LGMT, an open-source evaluation tool that detects self-contradictions in LLMs when identical logical queries are restated.

### [LLaCO: Continuous Diffusion for Large Language Models with Competition-Aware Semantic Scheduling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32604v1)

This preprint tackles trajectory misalignment in continuous text diffusion by proposing a competition-aware semantic scheduling technique for non-autoregressive text generation.

### [PHYRD: A Living Benchmark for Reproducing and Extending Theoretical Physics Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32575v1)

This preprint proposes a living benchmark for theoretical physics reasoning, evaluating LLM agents on both reproducing published results and discovering novel outcomes under modified conditions.

### [Pseudo-Labels as Rewards, Not Targets: Group-Relative Policy Optimization for Semi-Supervised ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32572v1)

This preprint demonstrates that using pseudo-labels as reward references in GRPO policy optimization avoids inheriting teacher errors during semi-supervised ASR domain adaptation.
