### [LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132v1)

A new paper explores how to leverage reinforcement learning to boost long-context reasoning capabilities in large language models without relying on heavy human annotation or external teacher models.

A new paper points out that interaction items in sequential recommendation vary drastically in their contribution, and proposes allocating computational budgets adaptively based on item importance.

A new paper addresses the challenge that world models struggle to perceive underlying physics properties like mass or friction, introducing in-context physics calibration to mitigate prediction drift.

A new paper introduces a dynamic agentic benchmark to evaluate how frontier LLMs perform as both creators and solvers within interactive environments.

A new paper reports on how AI assisted researchers in discovering A-greement, a novel low-latency consensus protocol for distributed computing and state machine replication.

### [Quant-Arena: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Quantitative Trading Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30113v1)

A new paper introduces Quant-Arena to benchmark whether large language models can complete the quantitative research loop from market hypothesis to backtesting.

### [Multimodal Embodied Agents: A Unified Capability-centric Perspective from Computer-Use to Robot-Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30108v1)

A new paper defines a unified capability-centric perspective for multimodal embodied agents, bridging computer-use agents and robotic systems under physical dynamics.
