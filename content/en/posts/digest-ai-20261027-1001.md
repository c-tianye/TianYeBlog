A new paper investigates the weight matrix rank compression mechanism during neural network grokking, tracking the relationship between rank reduction and generalization phase transition.

A new paper introduces FlowGenesis, a framework where neural architectures adapt through neuron birth and pruning guided by local activation representation flux.

A new paper explores continuous diffusion language modeling choices on the unit sphere using geodesic and von Mises-Fisher paths.

A new paper performs a mechanism-level diagnostic across various configurations to explain the merge instability of the GEPA prompt optimizer.

A new paper presents BriLLM-MetaPred as the first engineering implementation of the MetaPred machine learning framework instantiated for language modeling.

### [The Patient Is Always Right? Evaluating Sycophancy in Medical Diagnosis with Graded Patient Beliefs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34013v1)

A new paper evaluates sycophancy in nine AI models when processing incorrect medical beliefs presented by patients during diagnosis.

### [Reversible Pseudonymisation of Documents Sent to External LLMs: an Open Bilingual Benchmark and an Engine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34012v1)

A new paper introduces an open bilingual benchmark and engine for reversible pseudonymisation to safeguard documents sent to external language models.

A new paper introduces the Fed-Universe architecture to transform passive federated averaging into an active negotiation framework for decentralized alignment.
