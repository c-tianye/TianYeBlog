## Overview

This issue features a collection of new arXiv preprints diving deep into optimization strategies, LLM selection biases, instruction hierarchies, and security within agent ecosystems.

## Papers

### [Center-of-Mass Interpretation of Evolution Strategies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35865v1)

A new paper presents a geometrical interpretation of evolution strategies by treating perturbed models as particles and their rewards as masses to analyze update directions.

### [Preserve to Expand: Source-Aware Adaptation and Model Merging for Multilingual LLM-Based ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35846v1)

A new paper investigates adaptation and model merging strategies for multilingual speech LLMs to expand new languages while preserving previously supported ones without source-language data replay.

### [Understanding Jev-Style System One Models: Does Structure Reduce Selection Bias?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35826v1)

A new paper explores whether predicting probabilities directly over supplied candidates helps reduce selection bias in large language models.

### [Learning When to Commit: Schedule Distillation for Fast Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35804v1)

A new paper introduces schedule distillation to address the trade-off between decoding rounds and token prediction dependencies in diffusion language models.

### [Quantifying Claim Smoothing by LLM Research Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35793v1)

A new paper uncovers and quantifies the claim smoothing failure where long-form research reports generated by LLMs fail to accurately reflect underlying source documents.

### [Improving Instruction Hierarchy by Learning from Goal-Goal Conflicts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35093v1)

A new paper demonstrates that fine-tuning LLMs on simulated instruction conflict examples effectively improves instruction hierarchy and prevents policy violations.

### [CoSA: The Conflict of Skills Attack in Real-World LLM Agent Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35082v1)

A new paper analyzes the Conflict of Skills vulnerability in agent ecosystems, showing how isolated skill checks fail when capabilities are composed downstream.

### [Zero-Shot Multi-Class Wafer Map Classification with Rule-Ordered Prompts for Large Multimodal Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35080v1)

A new paper proposes a training-free framework combining connected components with rule-ordered prompts for zero-shot wafer map defect classification.

### [Toward Fast Human Reaction Modeling from High-Frequency Digital Interaction Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35079v1)

A new paper explores the modeling of rapid human reaction behaviors using high-resolution gameplay telemetry and a millisecond-precision visibility engine.

## Takeaway

These latest preprints highlight ongoing theoretical and empirical advancements across optimization algorithms, agent security, and specialized multimodal applications.
