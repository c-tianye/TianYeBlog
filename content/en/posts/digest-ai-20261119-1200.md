### [RSIBench-Infra: Can Agents Optimize Their Own Training and Inference Infrastructure at Production Scale?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24395v1)

A new paper introduces RSIBench-Infra, a benchmark designed to evaluate whether AI agents can achieve end-to-end performance gains by optimizing training and inference infrastructure at production scale.

### [Temporal Rank in Recurrent Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24394v1)

A new paper identifies an information bottleneck induced by rank-constrained hidden state dynamics in Recurrent Neural Networks, shedding light on their temporal modeling limits.

### [What Majority Labels Hide: Annotator Disagreement and Model Errors in Multi-Dialect Arabic Sexism Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24383v1)

A new paper presents MuDAS, a multi-dialect Arabic sexism detection dataset that preserves individual annotator disagreements to better evaluate model behavior on contested cases.

### [How to Turn an Attention Neural Network into an Interpretable Hard-Logic Predicate Circuit: Truth-Table Predicate Extraction and State-Machine Merging under Ship-of-Theseus Replacement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24381v1)

A new paper proposes converting a trained attention network into an interpretable hard-logic predicate circuit using truth-table predicate extraction and state-machine merging.

### [MoE-Context: Show the Model Only What It Needs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24379v1)

A new paper introduces MoE-Context, a mechanism that uses a fast pass to let language models read only the necessary context blocks at each step while retaining others as concise summary notes.

### [LLM-Assisted Multi-Fidelity Bayesian Optimization: Fusing Expert Knowledge with Numerical Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24377v1)

A new paper proposes a multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization method that fuses expert knowledge with numerical data to lower engineering design costs.

### [VAFL: A Vertical Analytic Federated Learning Mechanism for Efficient Crowdsourced Services via Closed-Form Solutions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24367v1)

A new paper introduces VAFL, a vertical analytic federated learning framework that fits classification and regression heads via regularized least squares on fixed local representations.

### [LoopCache: Accelerating Long-Context Self-Speculative Decoding for Looped Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24301v1)

A new paper presents LoopCache to accelerate long-context self-speculative decoding workflows in looped Transformers.

### [LipSpell: A Language-Independent Phonetic Scoring Framework for Spelling Correction Combining Distinctive-Feature Edit Distance, Keyboard Geometry, and Corpus Frequency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24285v1)

A new paper introduces LipSpell, a language-independent phonetic scoring framework combining distinctive-feature edit distance, keyboard geometry, and corpus frequency for spelling correction.

### [From a finite subgroup of $SO(3)$:$C_2$ leads to a Holant dichotomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24236v1)

A new paper investigates counting complexity within the Holant framework, establishing complexity dichotomy theorems derived from specific finite subgroups.

### [RoboBuilder: Agentic Robot Co-Design with Real-world Video Feedback in the Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23855v1)

A new paper introduces RoboBuilder, a benchmark where coding agents co-design robot hardware and programs with real-world physical course validation.

### [ReflectScene:MLLM-Guided Closed-Loop Diffusion for 3D Indoor Scene Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23845v1)

A new paper proposes ReflectScene, an MLLM-guided closed-loop diffusion framework for generating 3D indoor scenes that satisfy strict geometric and semantic constraints.
