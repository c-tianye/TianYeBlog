Addressing the tendency of high-resolution visual agents to hallucinate answers using commonsense priors rather than inspecting raw evidence, this preprint introduces a contrast-set policy optimization framework to enforce proper visual grounding.

Focusing on Model Context Protocol (MCP) ecosystems, this preprint examines the security implications of unverified tool metadata and quantifies the risks of provider impersonation.
