## Overview

This edition focuses exclusively on new preprint papers from arXiv, centered around agentic inference reuse and embodied decision-making. Researchers continue to explore ways to efficiently store reasoning traces, compress long-thought models to low precision, and let generalist architectures directly command physical robotic manipulators.

## Papers

### [On Agentic Manipulation Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892v1)

This preprint introduces Agentic Manipulation Policies (AMPs), demonstrating that generalist foundation models can directly command physical manipulators with higher reliability than specialized vision-language-action policies.

### [CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09891v1)

Addressing the computational waste of models solving common subproblems from scratch, this preprint proposes an agentic reasoning caching mechanism to re-apply previous reasoning trajectories on CPUs with minimal model overhead.

### [CodeQuant2: Enhanced Outlier Mitigation and Adaptive Clustering for Low-Precision LRMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09868v1)

This preprint identifies severe activation spikes in early reasoning decoding steps and proposes an outlier mitigation and adaptive clustering strategy to prevent degradation under aggressive low-bit quantization.

### [On the Reliability of Majority-Vote Pseudo-Labels in Test-Time Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09872v1)

This preprint analyzes majority-vote self-supervision in test-time reinforcement learning, decomposing errors into population bias and finite-rollout variance to evaluate when unsupervised adaptation actually helps.

### [FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09882v1)

Moving beyond deterministic multi-person tracking, this preprint presents a conditional flow matching formulation capable of capturing coordinated social dynamics and diverse plausible future trajectories.

## Also worth reading

- [Graded convolutional networks: scale invariance without the scale and robustness to unknown blur](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09878v1) — This preprint leverages graded vector spaces to achieve intrinsic scale invariance and blur robustness without explicit scale estimation.
- [GRAC: Graph-Based Retrieval-Augmented Captioning for Surgical Scene Description](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09866v1) — This preprint integrates clinical domain knowledge graphs with retrieval-augmented generation to automate descriptive surgical reporting.
- [Grasp-TAGS: Task-Aware Grasp Selection via Critic Distillation for Prehensile Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09864v1) — This preprint introduces critic distillation to select task-aware grasps for mobile manipulators coordinating long-horizon heavy object relocation.
- [Flow-Guided Target-Space Alignment via Path Consistency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09863v1) — This preprint enforces path consistency in flow models to preserve target geometry and cross-modal correspondences simultaneously.
