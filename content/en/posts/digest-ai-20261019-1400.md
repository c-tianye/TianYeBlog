## Overview

This edition highlights several recent arXiv preprints spanning generative model architectures, efficient LLM decoding, interpretability consistency, and autonomous scientific discovery workflows.

## Papers

### [Rethinking the design space of Vanilla Pixel Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23854v1)

This preprint investigates pixel-space diffusion models that bypass lossy latent bottlenecks, systematically rethinking architectural choices and prediction objectives for vanilla diffusion transformers operating directly on raw pixels.

### [Acceptance Is Not Speed: Length Inflation in Training-Free Lossy Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23818v1)

This paper identifies a length inflation phenomenon in relaxed, training-free speculative decoding, demonstrating that higher token acceptance rates under lossy verification can shift target output distributions and offset expected speedups.

### [Beyond SAEs: Consistent Concept Discovery with Sparse Coding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23801v1)

Addressing the instability of sparse autoencoder (SAE) dictionaries across independent runs, this preprint proposes Consistent Sparse Dictionary Learning (ConSDL) using sample-level sparse coding to deliver reproducible concept representations for foundation models.

### [Self-Building Labs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23826v1)

To overcome the rigidity of traditional self-driving labs that require extensive manual engineering for new procedures, this preprint introduces self-building laboratory frameworks designed to let AI systems autonomously reconfigure physical experimentation setups.
