## Overview

This issue features a collection of new AI preprints exploring advancements in language model fine-tuning, low-latency streaming inference, autonomous agent self-improvement, and cross-organization trust architectures.

## Papers

### [Ambient LLMs: Data-Efficient Supervised Fine-tuning via Temperature Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23229v1)

A new paper introduces Ambient SFT, using temperature matching to prevent overfitting and catastrophic forgetting during supervised fine-tuning on smaller datasets.

### [Spool: Low-Latency Streaming Inference for Hybrid Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23228v1)

A new paper presents Spool to achieve low-latency sliding window inference for hybrid vision-language models without high computational overhead.

### [StringQFT: A Multiuse Textbook Resource for AI-Assisted Theoretical Physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23225v1)

A new paper releases StringQFT as a comprehensive textbook collection and benchmark infrastructure to support AI-assisted theoretical physics.

### [An inexact stochastic variance-reduced gradient method for constrained convex finite-sum optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23221v1)

A new paper studies constrained convex finite-sum optimization problems and proposes a method to compute nearly feasible stochastic optimal solutions.

### [Do LLM Agents Still Need to Be Told How to Self-Improve?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23219v1)

A new paper investigates whether increasingly capable language model agents can autonomously determine their own self-improvement strategies.

### [What Does a Bias Head Measure? Non-Identifiability in Head-Level Bias Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23218v1)

A new paper audits attention head-level bias localization methods in large language models and uncovers fundamental non-identifiability issues.

### [Higher-Scoring Coding Models Did Not Get More Work Done](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23216v1)

A new paper analyzes real-world user data and finds that coding models with higher benchmark scores do not necessarily accomplish more actual work.

### [Reading Scene States: Uncovering Safety Failures in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23206v1)

A new paper identifies scene-state safety vulnerabilities in vision-language models and introduces SceneSafetyBench to measure harmful assistance risks.

### [Fine-Tuning Gemma 4 Edge Models for MATLAB Signal-Processing Code with SFT and QLoRA on a Laptop iGPU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23205v1)

A new paper explores fine-tuning edge language models using SFT and QLoRA on a laptop integrated GPU for offline MATLAB signal-processing code generation.

### [Agent Trust Infrastructure (ATI): A Federated Identity, Trust, Attestation, and Accountability Architecture for Cross-Organization Autonomous AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23202v1)

A new paper proposes the Agent Trust Infrastructure (ATI) to enable federated identity verification and secure collaboration for cross-organization AI agents.

### [Structure--Residual Inductive Biases for Language Model Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23197v1)

A new paper proposes a structure-residual principle as an explicit inductive bias for language model pretraining to separate structure from realization detail.

### [Polarity Composition Hypothesis: Causal Evidence from Negation in NLI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23185v1)

A new paper investigates how language models process negation in natural language inference and provides causal evidence for the polarity composition hypothesis.
