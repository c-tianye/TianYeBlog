## Papers

### [Density-Aware Reward Scaling: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Reward-Proportional Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04792v1)

A new paper introduces a density-aware reward processing mechanism to promote reward-proportional sampling in continuous outcome spaces while maintaining standard policy-gradient training.

### [ReSI-Math: Exploring Recoverable Capability Frontiers for Efficient Reasoning Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04787v1)

A new paper explores recoverable capability frontiers when models fail mathematical reasoning, revealing that direct failure does not necessarily imply an underlying inability.

### [Kierston & KierstonBench: Simulation of Allentown, PA and Evaluation of Language Models in Crime Investigation and Surveillance Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04785v1)

A new paper presents a city-scale simulation engine and benchmark to evaluate language models in crime investigation and surveillance tasks.

### [Reducing Logprob Without Causal Verification: Diagnosing SAE-Based PII Suppression in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04782v1)

A new paper diagnoses sparse autoencoder-based PII suppression interventions, showing that log-probability reduction cannot distinguish feature-specific suppression from magnitude artifacts.

### [Replica-Aware Split Inference for Edge IoT Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04779v1)

A new paper proposes a replica-aware recovery approach to handle device failures in split inference for edge Internet of Things systems.

### [TactiNavi: Serving and Revising an LLM Game Coach with Explicit Strategy Conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04772v1)

A new paper describes a serving system for an LLM game coach in a commercial open-world game that uses explicit strategy conditions across compilation and online execution.

### [When Should an LLM Steering Edit Depend on the Input?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04757v1)

A new paper examines when activation steering edits should depend on the input and proposes conditioning them on requested classes.

### [TAPES: Task-Aligned Joint Predictive Pretraining for Transaction Event Sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04747v1)

A new paper introduces a task-aligned joint predictive pretraining method for transaction event sequences to handle diverse prediction tasks in commerce and finance.

### [When Ground Truth Is Not Ground Truth: Automatic Labeling for Hallucination Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04697v1)

A new paper investigates the limitations of automatic labeling methods when evaluating hallucination detectors in large language models.

### [CardioVascBench:Prioritized Diagnosis Generation from Real-World Chinese Cardiovascular Records](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04687v1)

A new paper introduces a retrospective benchmark derived from real-world Chinese cardiovascular inpatient records for prioritized diagnosis generation.

### [MIMIC-Note-Extract: A Large-Scale Structured Dataset Extracted from MIMIC-IV-Note Discharge Summaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04685v1)

A new paper releases a large-scale structured dataset extracted from free-text discharge summaries to facilitate electronic health record research.

### [Self-Privileged Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04632v1)

A new paper explores token-level weight introductions in supervised fine-tuning to better understand task adaptation and post-training foundations.
