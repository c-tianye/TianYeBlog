## Overview

## Papers

### [HiVe-Mem: Interleaving Horizontal Memory Expansion and Vertical Evidence Composition for Multimodal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12872v1)

A new paper introduces HiVe-Mem, a memory framework for multimodal agents that interleaves horizontal expansion with vertical evidence composition. The approach improves retrieval precision and long-context reasoning over extended interaction histories.

### [WorkflowArena: Benchmarking Executable Workflow Orchestration in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12856v1)

A new paper proposes WorkflowArena to benchmark how effectively LLM agents instantiate and orchestrate executable multi-agent workflows. Results demonstrate that system-level organizational quality significantly impacts overall task success.

### [How Well Does AI Peer Review Work? A Benchmark Built from 100 Planted Errors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12820v1)

A new paper evaluates frontier models and AI peer review tools by planting 100 errors across psychology papers. While top single systems caught 71 errors, ensembling outputs pushed the catch rate up to 93%.

### [Agentic-OPD: Recovering Forgotten Agent Capabilities via On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12799v1)

A new paper identifies capability loss when keeping only the best-validation checkpoint during RL training of LLM agents. The authors introduce Agentic-OPD, using on-policy distillation to recover solved task capabilities from earlier checkpoints.
