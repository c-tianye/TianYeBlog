### [ClueFlow: Learning Long-Horizon Search through Attributed Research-State Transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25980v1)

A new paper introduces ClueFlow, which learns long-horizon search from attributed research-state transitions to better integrate evidence during complex exploration.

### [GraphEvoResearcher: Graph-Aware Training-Free Self-Reflective Reasoning for Cost-Bounded Multi-Hop Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25978v1)

A new paper presents GraphEvoResearcher, a training-free, graph-aware self-reflective reasoning protocol for cost-bounded multi-hop question answering.

### [On the Benefits of Uncertainty Estimation for Species Classification: A Case Study on Optimizing Expert re-Annotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25977v1)

A new paper investigates how calibrated confidence estimates support selective classification to reduce expert workloads in species identification tasks.

### [Correctness-Dependent Asymmetric Target Distributions for Self-Distillation Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25973v1)

A new paper proposes Asymmetric Policy Optimization via Self-Distillation (APSD) to improve post-training supervision by constructing correctness-dependent targets.

### [GLOBE: Geometry-Led Online Behavioral Early Exit via Unit-Sphere Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25978v1)

A new paper introduces GLOBE, an online, training-free algorithm that terminates chain-of-thought generation early using unit-sphere trajectories.

### [Reconstruction and Discrete-Event Simulation Assessment of an Analytical](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25967v1)

A new paper presents an analytical-DES benchmark for intra-aisle robotic picking in automated warehousing.

### [Behavioral Probes for Information Flow in LLM Swarms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25966v1)

A new paper studies LLM swarm interactions from an information-flow-based perspective and introduces behavioral probes to quantify interaction patterns.

### [ABACUS: Program-Guided Multistep Reasoning over Frozen Judgment Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25963v1)

A new paper introduces ABACUS, a framework that constructs judgment schemas and composition programs for multistep reasoning over frozen judgment models.
